# Commercial Use

Video Craft is open source under AGPL-3.0. The community edition is designed to be useful, inspectable, and modifiable.

Commercial support and licensing are available separately for teams that need private deployment, closed-source integration, or a customized production pipeline.

## Available Commercial Work

- private production-pipeline deployment
- team-specific style and theme library adaptation
- brand-template and overlay-system construction
- custom validators and delivery quality gates
- private review dashboards and approval pipelines
- internal training for agent-assisted editing and compositing workflows
- closed-source commercial licensing

## When To Contact

Contact the maintainer if you want to:

- integrate Video Craft into a closed-source product;
- offer a hosted service based on a modified version;
- build a private company version with non-public themes, templates, or scripts;
- use the project name, author identity, or brand in commercial promotion;
- commission a custom video production pipeline for a team or client.
